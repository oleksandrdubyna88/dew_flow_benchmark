# Group brief — pr-diff

Questions anchored to a CHANGE: what a pull request altered, and why.

These are the questions with the strongest seed, because a merge date is a real date. That makes the group
the backbone of the memorisation check — a question seeded after a subject's training cutoff cannot have been
memorised, and this is the only group where that is reliably knowable.

- `seedKind` is `pr` and `seedReference` is the PR number or url; `seedAt` is its MERGE date.
- Ask what behaviour changed, what the change replaced, or which member the change moved the logic into.
- The `Member` expectation points at the code AFTER the change, at the pinned commit.
- If you cannot establish a real merge date, do not invent one — write a question for another group instead.
