## Goal

Prove, through the real service (`PanelService.AskConsultantsAsync`) with a played shim (`PlayedKimi`), that a Kimi `disk` question row
is launched in a COPY of its granted root, its prompt names the copy and never the root, and after every ending — answered, refused before
the launch, cancelled mid-turn — its one-shot home folder `<homes>/coai-vh/<8>` and its copy are gone, junction-first (the deleter's unlink
recorder is never invoked), and the fake person's folder is unchanged. **Decision 5: nothing new in `QuestionRowLaunch`** — this task writes a
test; it changes `QuestionRowLaunch.cs` ONLY if an arm is red for a production reason (then: the smallest fix, and say why in the summary).
Delivers **S5.6.7**.
