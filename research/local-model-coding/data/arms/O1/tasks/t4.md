## Goal
Prove, through the real `QuestionRowLaunch` with `PlayedKimi`, that a Kimi `disk` row's prompt names the COPIES and never the root, and
that after every ending (answered; refused before the launch; cancelled mid-turn) the row's scratch and its `coai-vh/<8>` folder are
gone, junction-first, the fake person's folder intact. **Decision 5: nothing new in `QuestionRowLaunch`** — this task is tests only,
unless a scenario is red for a production reason (then a minimal fix in `QuestionRowLaunch.cs`, reported with its reason).
Scenario: **S5.6.7**.
