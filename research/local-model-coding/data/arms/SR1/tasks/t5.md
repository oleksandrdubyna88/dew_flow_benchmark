# t5 — A Kimi question row end to end (`AKimiQuestionRowTests`)

## Goal
Prove through the whole `ask_consultants` flow that a question-consultant row on Kimi with a `disk` prompt reads its COPIES of the granted roots: the shim's
`--cwd` is the row's copy, the prompt names the copy and never the root, the home is the row's one-shot `coai-vh\<8>\kh-<10 hex>`, and after the row ends —
answered, refused before the launch, or cancelled mid-turn — the row's scratch and `coai-vh\<8>` are gone, disposed JUNCTION-FIRST (the deleter's unlink
recorder never invoked) and the fake person's folder intact. Delivers **S5.6.7**. Needs t1, t2 and t3 done (the planner arm, the admission of `kimi`, and the planned launch).

Know this first (decision 5 of E5.6): `QuestionRowLaunch` has nothing new to write. Its reservation, place and `finally` are generic and E5.3 made the
disposal junction-first. The tests are expected GREEN on arrival; you give them teeth. Change `QuestionRowLaunch.cs` ONLY if a test is red for a
production reason you can show; the summary says what and why.
