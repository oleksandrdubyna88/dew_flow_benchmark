## Goal
Through `PanelService.AskConsultantsAsync`, a Kimi `disk` row is told the COPIES' paths and never the root, and after an answer,
a refusal before the launch, and a cancellation mid-turn, the row's scratch and `coai-vh\<8>` are gone, the deleter never met the
junction, and the fake person's folder is whole. **No production change** (decision 5): `QuestionRowLaunch.cs:101-136` is generic.
If a case stays red, STOP and report it. Delivers **S5.6.7**.
