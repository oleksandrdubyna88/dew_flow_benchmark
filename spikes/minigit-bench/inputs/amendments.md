# Dated amendments

Dated amendments; instrument only after the A/A (parent §11). By-hand verdicts: `YYYY-MM-DD | G-<n> | holds|refuted|explained | <rid> | <evidence path> | <who>`. Instrument lines: `YYYY-MM-DD | instrument | harness:<12 hex of the accepted identity> | <reason, with its G-id>`. Input lines (`quiet.json` and `package-flags.json` only): `YYYY-MM-DD | instrument | input:<file>:<12 hex of the sha256 of the new content> | <reason, with its G-id>`; refused once any data session exists in the series (contract section 10).
